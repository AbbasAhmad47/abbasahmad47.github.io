Privacy Policy — Tycoon Khata

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Tycoon Khata ("the App") is developed by Tycoon Digital ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google (Firebase Authentication). This is used only to identify your account.
  • Your business data. The records you create in the App — customers, khata/ledger entries, stock items, invoices, and related notes — are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your own account so they can back up and sync across your devices.
  • Profile information you provide. Shop name and optional profile details you enter in the App. A profile photo you pick is stored on your device only.
  • Device/security information. If you enable fingerprint or PIN lock, that setting stays on your device. We do not receive your fingerprint data — it never leaves your device.

How We Use Information

  • To run the App's features: khata management, stock, invoices, backup and restore.
  • To sync your data to your own devices when you sign in with the same Google account.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use your business data for advertising.

Data Sharing

  • Your data is stored with Google Firebase services (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which process it on our behalf.
  • We do not share your personal or business data with any other third party, except where required by law.

Data Security

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Cloud access rules ensure that a signed-in user can access only their own data.
  • The App's fingerprint/PIN lock adds protection on your device.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your data remains stored while your account is active so your backup stays available.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at the contact below. We will delete the data from our cloud within 30 days of a verified request.

Children's Privacy

The App is a business tool intended for adult shopkeepers and traders. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App's features change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
