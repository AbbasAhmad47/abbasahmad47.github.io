About us

A small agency with a builder's mindset

Tycoon Digital is a digital agency based in Pakistan, established in 2025. We started in digital marketing — helping businesses present themselves properly online — and grew into software, because our clients kept asking for the same thing: systems that make the daily work easier.

Today we do both. We market your business to bring customers in, and we build the apps and systems — Android apps, ERP and CRM — that help you manage those customers, your stock and your money. Our own product, Tycoon Khata, is used by wholesale traders to run their shops.

We keep things simple: honest advice, clean work, and software that fits your business — not the other way around.
